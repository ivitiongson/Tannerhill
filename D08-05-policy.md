## Warranty

**Twelve months from the delivery date on everything.** Two years on cast iron, because I have been saying two years on cast iron since 2023 and I am not taking that back now.

**We replace it if it failed in normal use inside the window.** We do not argue, we do not ask for a receipt if the order is in our system, and we do not make somebody send the item back first.

**Not covered:**

- Rust. Cast iron rusts if it is washed with soap or put away damp. We say so on the product page and in the care card, and if we keep replacing rusted pans we will do it forever.
- Accidental damage. Dropped, run over, left in a fire longer than anybody should.
- Normal wear. Scratches, seasoning wearing thin, handles loosening with use.
- Anything outside the window, with one exception below.

**The exception.** If somebody is inside fifteen months and they are polite and it is clearly a manufacturing problem, replace it and do not ask me. I would rather lose the cost of a grill than an Amazon review. Past fifteen months, the answer is no.

## What we ask the customer for, before we decide anything

No claim gets a yes or a no until we have these four. If one is missing, we ask for it and we say why.

1. **The order number**, or enough for us to find the order. Name and delivery ZIP code will usually do it.
2. **When it arrived.** Not when they ordered. The twelve months runs from delivery, and on Kickstarter those are months apart.
3. **Photos.** The fault itself, and one of the whole item. For cast iron, a photo of the cooking surface.
4. **What happened, in their words.** How it was being used when it failed, and for cast iron, how they have been washing and storing it.

## What we check against

In this order. Stop at the first one that gives you an answer.

| Check | Where to look | What it settles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is it inside the window | Delivery date on the order, against twelve months, or two years for cast iron | Outside the window and past fifteen months, the answer is no |
| Is it on the excluded list | Their description and the photos | Rust, accidental damage or normal wear, the answer is no |
| Does the photo match the description | The photos against what they told us | If it does not, ask once more before deciding |
| Is it a repeat | Search the inbox for their email | Third claim from the same customer in a year comes to me |

If all four checks pass and it is inside the window, replace it. Do not ask me.

If two people could look at the same claim and reach different answers, that is an edge case and it goes to Marco.
