# Proposed warranty change

**Status: Pending Lena's approval. Not in force.**

## Proposed

- Orders placed on or after [DATE]: 12-month warranty on all items.
- Orders placed before [DATE]: the skillet keeps its 2-year warranty.

## Current policy (D08-05, effective 18 March 2026)

- 12 months from the **delivery** date on everything.
- 2 years on cast iron.

## Differences

a) Cast iron drops from 2 years to 12 months on new orders.
b) The proposal counts from the **order** date. D08-05 counts from the **delivery** date.
c) The proposal names only "the skillet". It doesn't say what happens to other cast-iron items (e.g. the Dutch oven) ordered before [DATE].

## Open questions for Lena

1. What is [DATE]?
2. Should the window run from the order date or the delivery date?
3. Does "the skillet" mean all cast iron, or only the skillet?
