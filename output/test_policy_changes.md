# Proposed warranty change

**Status: Pending Lena's approval. Not in force.**

D08-05 is the policy in force (effective 18 Mar 2026). Nothing below changes how a message is sorted today.

## The two rules side by side

| | Current (D08-05) | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Window, most items | 12 months from delivery | 12 months on all items, for orders on/after [DATE] |
| Window, cast iron | 2 years from delivery, on all cast iron | 12 months, for orders on/after [DATE] |
| Older orders | n/a | Orders before [DATE]: the skillet keeps 2 years |
| What starts the clock | Delivery date | Order date decides which rule applies (on/after or before [DATE]) |

## Differences

1. **Cast iron drops from 2 years to 12 months** for orders on/after [DATE]. D08-05 says: "I have been saying two years on cast iron since 2023 and I am not taking that back now."
2. **Order date vs delivery date.** D08-05 runs every window from delivery: "Not when they ordered... on Kickstarter those are months apart." The proposal uses the order date to pick the rule.
3. **"Skillet" vs all cast iron.** D08-05 gives 2 years to all cast iron. The proposal protects only the skillet on older orders. Other cast iron (for example the Dutch oven) is not mentioned.

## Open questions for Lena

1. What is [DATE]?
2. For orders on/after [DATE], does the 12 months still run from delivery, or from the order date?
3. For orders before [DATE], do other cast iron items (Dutch oven and any others) keep 2 years, or drop to 12 months? The proposal only names the skillet.
4. Kickstarter orders: an order can be placed months before delivery. Is a Kickstarter pledge's "order date" the pledge date?
5. Does the 15-month exception (polite, clearly a manufacturing problem) still apply to cast iron under the new rule?
6. The website warranty page and Amazon listing bullets already differ from D08-05. Should they be updated once, after this decision, rather than twice?
7. Customers already told "two years on cast iron": does anything change for them?
