    What this page does

    • Shows the last sort (category, owner, missing info, flags) for every message in sources/.
    • Recalculates waiting time, urgency and days open each time the page opens.
    • Lets a person close a case, or log that a reply was sent, in this browser.

    What it never does

    • Never drafts, suggests, queues or sends a reply. A person writes every reply.
    • Never approves or denies a warranty claim, return or refund.
    • Never changes an order, a refund, a price or a source file.
    • Never closes a case on its own. Only a person fills in "Closed by" and "Closed date".

    When unsure

    • It never picks the most likely answer. It writes "UNSURE: reason" and flags it.
    • Unclear request → Marco. Two topics with different owners → "UNSURE: X or Y", Marco.
    • Warranty or return edge case → Marco (D08-05). Lena only if Marco isn't sure.
    • Shipping damage → Lena (carrier vs us not decided). Wholesale → Lena, quote nothing.
    • Threat (review, A-to-Z, chargeback, legal) → THREAT flag, Lena. Urgency is not changed.
    • Product detail not in sources → "UNSURE: not in sources", Priya.

    Who is in charge

    • Marco: order status and shipping; edge cases (D08-05).
    • Lena: warranty, returns, refunds, billing, shipping damage, wholesale, threats.
    • Priya: pre-sale and product questions, Kickstarter.
    • Ivs: anything that conflicts with CLAUDE.md, or a new folder in sources/.

    Urgency bands (response time only)

    • Weekday hours only (Saturday and Sunday removed). Target is 12 hours.
    • Normal 0-12 h · High over 12, up to 36 h · Very High over 36 h.
    • Clock starts at the customer's first email after our last reply (same order number or same sender). It stops at our first reply. No reply: it runs to now, shown as "waiting, no reply".
    • No reply data in sources and none logged → "UNSURE". Hours since the clock started are shown in grey.

    Case opened and days open

    • Case opened = the customer's earliest email in the same conversation found in sources/.
    • If they say they wrote before and that email isn't in the pack: this message's time + "(UNSURE: earlier emails not in pack)".
    • Days open = calendar days (weekends included), one decimal, to the closed date or to now.

    Missing info (warranty and returns, D08-05)

    • Order number, or name + delivery ZIP.
    • When it arrived (delivery date, not order date).
    • Photos: the fault and the whole item; cooking surface for cast iron.
    • What happened, in their words; for cast iron, washing and storage.
