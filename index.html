What the sorter does

Does
Sorts each message by category, person in charge, missing info, flags, urgency and days open.
Never
Drafts or sends a reply · changes an order, refund or price · approves or denies a claim · closes a case · edits its own rules.
When unsure
Writes "UNSURE: reason" and flags it to a named person. Never picks the most likely answer.
In charge
Kickstarter channel → Priya · wholesale → Lena · everything else → Marco. Anyone can reassign a case to Lena, Marco or Priya on the Inbox tab. Money, shipping damage and third warranty claims carry a NOTE flag, because D08-04 and D08-05 send those to Lena.
Urgency
Weekday hours only, from the customer's first email after our last reply to our first reply (or to the end of the pack if no reply).
Low: Responded we have replied · Normal not responded, 0–12 h · High not responded, more than 12, up to 36 h · Very High not responded, more than 36 h
Threats sit in Flags and never change urgency.
Follow-up replies aren't recorded yet, so check Low: Responded cases before closing them.
Case opened
Date and time of the customer's first email in the conversation.
Days open
Calendar days from Case opened until a person closes the case.
Missing info
Warranty and returns only (D08-05): order number (or name + delivery ZIP) · delivery date · photos (fault + whole item; cooking surface for cast iron) · what happened (cast iron: washing and storage too).
Flag to
Unclear request → Marco (cover Lena) · two owners → Marco (cover Lena) · warranty/return edge case → Marco (cover Lena) · shipping damage → Lena (cover Marco) · wholesale → Lena · threat → Lena (cover Marco) · product detail not in sources → Priya (cover Lena).
